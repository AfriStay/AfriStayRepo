1. Data Flow Diagram (textual)

3. Outstanding Item

Row 1 above (Supabase) is the primary basis for this application. AfriStay should obtain Supabase's standard Data Processing Agreement (available via the Supabase Dashboard under organization/legal settings, or by request to Supabase's support/legal team) and attach it as the "Hosting agreement(s) with service provider(s)" item required by Section 5 of the NCSA Application Form, alongside the already-signed Resend DPA.
